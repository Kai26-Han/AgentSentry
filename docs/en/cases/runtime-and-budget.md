## Question and controls

Individually valid calls can form risky probing, repeated denials, uncertain retries or excessive writes. Gateway history/state/atomic budgets can step up, deny or pause, with approval rechecks. Three denials in ten minutes is an engineering threshold, not an OWASP mandate. Goal clues/Judge add no authority. Pending/unknown write budget is not refunded merely because success was unconfirmed.
